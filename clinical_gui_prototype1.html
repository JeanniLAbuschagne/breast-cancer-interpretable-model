What the model thinks

Based on six nucleus features. The model flags anything above 40% probability as malignant. That cut-off was chosen on the training data, not the test set.

The measurements

These values come from the image analysis. You can drag the sliders to see what would change the model's view. The tick marks show where benign tends to be here (lighter) and malignant (darker) cases sit.

Why it thinks that

Each bar shows how much a measurement pushed the result toward benign or malignant. Longer bars mean that feature mattered more for this case.
